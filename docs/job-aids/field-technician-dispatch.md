# Job Aid: Field Technician Dispatch

Audience: Field technicians

Goal: Follow the day’s route from the depot through completion.

## Start of day

1. Open the app on phone or tablet.
2. Sign in with your approved Google account.
3. The app opens in **Field Mode**.
4. Review **Today’s Route**.
5. Check:
   - **Stops**
   - **Waiting**
   - **Arrived**
6. Tap **Open Full Route in Google Maps** if available.
7. Start from the depot and follow the route order.

## At each stop

1. Find the next customer.
2. Review treatment and address.
3. Open **Instructions** if present.
4. Tap **Navigate**.
5. Use **Call** or **Text** if needed.
6. Tap **Arrived** when onsite.

## Completing a stop

1. Tap **Completed**.
2. Confirm **Application Date**.
3. Select **Applicator**.
4. Add notes if needed.
5. Review chemical amounts.
6. Update **Actual** amount if different.
7. Tap **Complete and Save Application**.
8. Continue to next stop.

## End of day

- Confirm all completed stops were saved.
- Tell the office about skipped or incomplete stops.
- Report address issues, gate codes, or customer notes.
