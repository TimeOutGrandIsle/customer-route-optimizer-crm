# Job Aid: Create a New Treatment

Audience: Admin/office users

Goal: Add a treatment type, assign chemicals, and set per-acre rates.

## Before you start

- Confirm chemical products exist in the product library.
- Know the treatment window and target date.
- Know product rates per acre.

## Step 1: Open Treatment Builder

1. Open the app.
2. Choose **Office Mode**.
3. Open the **Treatments** tab.
4. Select **Treatment Builder**.
5. In **Treatment Types**, choose **Create New Treatment**.

## Step 2: Enter treatment details

1. Enter **Treatment Name**.
2. Enter **Description**.
3. Enter **Season Window Start** as `MM-DD`.
4. Enter **Season Window End** as `MM-DD`.
5. Enter **Target Month**.
6. Enter **Target Day**.
7. Check **Include in standard customer schedule** if appropriate.
8. Check **Treatment is active**.
9. Add **Internal Notes** if needed.
10. Click **Save Treatment**.

## Step 3: Assign chemicals and rates

1. Scroll to **Treatment Chemicals and Rates**.
2. Choose the treatment.
3. Select products under **Products**.
4. Enter **Rate Per Acre** and **Unit**.
5. Click **Save Chemicals and Rates**.

## Step 4: Verify

1. Confirm the success message.
2. Use **Chemical Mixture Calculator** to test a sample property.
3. Confirm quantities look reasonable.
