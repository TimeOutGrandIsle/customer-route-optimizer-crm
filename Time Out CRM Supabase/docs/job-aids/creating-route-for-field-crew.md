# Job Aid: Create a Route for a Field Crew

Audience: Office/admin users

Goal: Schedule due treatment visits, generate a route, and download/print the route files for the crew.

## Before you start

- Sign in with an admin or office account.
- Confirm customers, treatments, products, and applicators have already been imported or created.
- Confirm the depot address is correct in Settings.
- Confirm customer addresses are complete enough for routing.

## Step 1: Schedule treatment visits

1. Open the app.
2. In the sidebar, choose **Office Mode**.
3. Open the **Scheduling** tab.
4. Set **Service Date** to the date the crew will work.
5. Set **Number of Crews**.
6. Review **Due by Date**, **Already Queued**, and **Ready to Schedule**.
7. Use **Select All** or manually check the **Schedule** box for visits to add.
8. Click **Add Treatment(s) to Dispatch**.
9. Confirm the success message.

## Step 2: Preview crew routes

1. Stay on the **Scheduling** tab.
2. Scroll to **Crew Route Preview**.
3. Click **Preview Routes for Queued Jobs**.
4. Review each crew’s summary and manifest.

## Step 3: Generate the route

1. Open the **Dispatch** tab.
2. Review **Queued Jobs**.
3. Click **Generate Optimized Route**.
4. Review **Optimized Route**, route metrics, and **Driver Manifest**.
5. Click **Open Route in Google Maps** if available.

## Step 4: Download and print route files

### Printable daily route

1. Open the **Reports** tab.
2. Choose **Daily Route**.
3. Set **Route Date**.
4. Click **Download Printable Daily Route**.
5. Open the downloaded `.html` file.
6. Print from the browser.

### CSV files

1. Open the **Dispatch** tab.
2. Scroll to **Daily Load Sheet**.
3. Confirm:
   - **Water Gallons Per Acre**
   - **Large Tank Gallons**
   - **Small Tank Gallons**
4. Download:
   - **Download Chemical Totals CSV**
   - **Download Tank Plan CSV**
   - **Download Mix Summary CSV**

## Quality check

- Stops are assigned for the correct date.
- Route begins from the correct depot.
- Customer addresses look complete.
- Treatments are attached to stops.
- Chemicals/rates appear on the load sheet.
