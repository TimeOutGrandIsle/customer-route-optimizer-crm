# Job Aid: Generate a Daily Loadout Report

Audience: Office/admin users and crew leads

Goal: Produce the chemical and tank loading information needed for the day.

## Before you start

- Treatment visits must be scheduled and queued.
- Treatments must have chemicals and rates assigned.
- Customer square footage should be entered.

## Steps

1. Open the app.
2. Choose **Office Mode**.
3. Open the **Dispatch** tab.
4. Scroll to **Daily Load Sheet**.
5. Enter or confirm:
   - **Water Gallons Per Acre**
   - **Large Tank Gallons**
   - **Small Tank Gallons**
6. Review:
   - **Scheduled Stops**
   - **Total Acres**
   - **Total Water**
7. Open **Treatment Mix Totals**.
8. Open **Total Chemicals Needed**.
9. Open **Tank Load Breakdown**.
10. Download:
    - **Download Printable Load Sheet**
    - **Download Chemical Totals CSV**
    - **Download Tank Plan CSV**
    - **Download Mix Summary CSV**

## Printing

1. Click **Download Printable Load Sheet**.
2. Open the downloaded `.html` file.
3. Print from the browser.

## Troubleshooting

- No loads shown: confirm jobs were scheduled and queued.
- Missing chemicals: assign products/rates under **Treatments**.
- Amounts look wrong: check customer square footage and treatment rate per acre.
